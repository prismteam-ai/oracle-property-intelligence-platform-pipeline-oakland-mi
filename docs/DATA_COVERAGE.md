# Data coverage and claim boundaries

Accepted baseline: 490,775 parcels, 559,701 official site addresses, 559,708 parcel/address links and 490,799 canonical properties. Selected-layer completeness is separate from all-source county completeness. The bounded source inventory covers 63 communities with per-source access, rights, dates, counts and limitations in the operator evidence.

The current accepted protected companion has 39,282 normalized permit observations, 3,747 reported-owner observations and 669 building-year observations across 659 distinct nonconflicting properties. Source capture and observation dates remain distinct from the release date. A future reviewed refresh must update exact manifest/digest and counts; source freshness is not inferred from a redeploy.

`ISSUED` does not imply current open status. A provider-reported active status requires a documented mapping and fresh source evidence. Age since issue, observed current active state and uninterrupted historical open duration are distinct claims. Building year is a low-confidence roof proxy. Partial reported sale dates do not prove ten-year continuous ownership. Contractor applicants, listed businesses and verified licensees remain separate identities. BBB absence is unavailable, never zero.

The two historical immutable public roots are retained with exact manifests and recorded independent operator gateway proofs. Prepared later roots are not publicly published without separate authorization. Protected/provider/private records are excluded from public exports and bulk endpoints. See [limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) and [historical publication design](architecture/RUN_HISTORY_PUBLICATION.md).
