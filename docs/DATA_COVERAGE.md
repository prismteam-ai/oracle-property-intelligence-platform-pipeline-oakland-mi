## Reading the fields

`ISSUED` is not `OPEN`. Native status, documented provider activity, age since issue and uninterrupted open duration are distinct. Building year is a disclosed low-confidence roof-age proxy, not an installation date. A current-PIN index gap is not proof of unchanged ownership. Applicants, listed businesses and verified job contractors are separate identities; unavailable BBB is never zero. [Known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) records the accepted free-source outcome and all eight unmet original positive-source rows.
