# Data coverage and claim boundaries

Accepted baseline: 490,775 parcels, 559,701 official site addresses, 559,708 parcel/address links and 490,799 canonical properties. Selected-layer completeness is separate from all-source county completeness. The bounded source inventory covers 63 communities with per-source access, rights, dates, counts and limitations in the operator evidence.

The historical v7 protected companion retains 39,282 normalized permit observations, 3,747 reported-owner observations and 669 building-year observations across 659 distinct nonconflicting properties. Source capture and observation dates remain distinct from the release date. Its nine files are preserved byte-for-byte in the proposed v8 refresh. Source freshness is not inferred from a redeploy.

`ISSUED` does not imply current open status. A provider-reported active status requires a documented mapping and fresh source evidence. Age since issue, observed current active state and uninterrupted historical open duration are distinct claims. Building year is a low-confidence roof proxy. Partial reported sale dates do not prove ten-year continuous ownership. Contractor applicants, listed businesses and verified licensees remain separate identities. BBB absence is unavailable, never zero.

The two historical immutable public roots are retained with exact manifests and recorded independent operator gateway proofs. Prepared later roots are not publicly published without separate authorization. Protected/provider/private records are excluded from public exports and bulk endpoints. See [limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md) and [historical publication design](architecture/RUN_HISTORY_PUBLICATION.md).

API/private contract `0.5.0` adds protected, source-qualified observations without rewriting the historical public snapshot schema, hashes, CARs, CIDs, or four public run receipts. The bounded P6 acquisition captured 133 PermitStack roofing records; 131 have accepted property joins, two are held. Fifty-one associate with existing municipal permits and 80 remain additional provider source candidates. All 133 captured statuses are `ISSUED`; the accepted current-active cohort is zero. Provider classifications, activity mapping, observation dates, issue-age basis and original municipal facts remain separate. Two provider ROOFING classifications differ from the retained municipal-work projection kind=other; broad source permit types and work text remain unchanged. Provider-qualified candidates may include these two; this does not establish independent native roofing classification.

Sixteen official current-PIN index queries yielded 13 qualified observations, five supporting a strict more-than-ten-year no-observed-deed gap through **2026-10-19**, with three collapsed multi-PIN associations held. The source reports indexed-through 2026-10-20 and verified-through 2026-10-19. The global advertised 1964 start is not proven coverage of historic current-PIN lineage. All result pages and empty other filters qualify the current-PIN search; predecessor parcels and legal ownership continuity remain unestablished.

The private refresh receipt counts 149 source record/query units processed, 144 admitted source observations and five held units. Its 428 indexed evidence rows are not 428 transfers or properties. Public GIS bytes and canonical property counts remain unchanged. New runtime acceptance and exact v8 release pins require the separate native/query and release verification receipts; acquisition alone does not establish a deployed release.
